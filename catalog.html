Store description

Surface-mounted light fixtures can be installed on the ceiling, floor or walls to match your design concept. These fixtures have a wide range of uses. They are successfully used to light any space: offices, shopping and business centers, stores, HoReCa interiors and more.

Our catalog offers a wide selection of surface-mounted light fixtures. You will find fixtures of various shapes, colors, sizes, styles and more. By setting the characteristics you need, it is easy to choose the right model. However, to buy surface-mounted fixtures for a specific task, it is more convenient to contact our managers. Our specialists will create a professional lighting design, select the equipment and arrange delivery if needed. We offer surface-mounted fixtures for decorative, directional and flood lighting. Models with various light sources are available, but the widest choice is among LED fixtures.
